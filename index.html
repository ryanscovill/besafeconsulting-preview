Food safety

FOODSAFE Level 1

A one-day course for anyone who works with food. It covers foodborne illness, receiving and storing food, safe preparation and service, cleaning and sanitizing, and personal hygiene. The day ends with a written exam.

FOODSAFE Level 2

Food safety management for supervisors, managers, and owners. You'll learn how to build a food safety plan, train staff, and work with your health inspector. You need Level 1 first.

About Dean

Dean Scovill started in public health in 1989, working for Health Canada in northern British Columbia. His job was overseeing drinking water and wastewater systems for First Nations communities, where he saw first-hand how much a community depends on its operators.

He spent the next 21 years as an Environmental Health Officer in the Lower Mainland, 15 of them as a drinking water officer with Fraser Health. In 2013 he moved to the City of Surrey's Water Operations, where he became an Engineering Project Coordinator and helped several staff members earn their operator certification.

Today Dean works as an educational consultant, instructor, and curriculum developer for the water and wastewater industry. He has presented at BCWWA and Canadian Water and Wastewater Association conferences.

That field experience shapes how he teaches. When Dean explains why a chlorine residual matters at the far end of a long water main, or why a walk-in cooler has to stay at 4°C, he's drawing on things he has seen on the job.
